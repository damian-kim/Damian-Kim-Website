# Tidal Lattice

Tidal Lattice treats a galaxy as a negotiation between continuity and quantization. The spiral is not drawn as a symbol; it emerges from thousands of discrete rigid bodies sampling a density wave in three-dimensional space. Each cube occupies a precise seeded position, yet the accumulated field reads as something fluid, vast, and gravitational. The composition should feel like a meticulously crafted algorithm whose apparent inevitability hides countless hours of spatial tuning.

The disk is layered rather than flat. Its warm, dense core swells vertically while the outer arms thin into long strands, gaps, and isolated fragments. Distance controls scale, scatter, color, and luminosity so depth is legible through parallax before the camera moves. This balance of mass and negative space must show the restraint of deep computational expertise: controlled chaos, never undirected noise.

Two timescales animate the system. A slow global rotation carries the spiral as one celestial structure, while every cube tumbles around its own seeded axis and rate. Their local motion makes the field shimmer without dissolving its silhouette. The implementation should feel painstakingly optimized and master-level—thousands of independent lives expressed in a single instanced GPU draw rather than brute-force object updates.

Light originates conceptually from the core and is caught by the rotating faces. Warm illumination falls away with radius while a cooler directional light describes the remote arms. Fog, rim light, and sparse background stars conceal the boundary of the volume. Camera interaction reveals the construction deliberately: edge-on views expose the thin disk, while oblique views reveal the tidal warp and central bulge.

Seeded variation is the movement's memory. Every seed produces a reproducible galaxy with different arm fractures, orientations, and bright condensations, while the governing physics-inspired ratios remain stable. Beauty belongs to the algorithm in motion, not a privileged still frame—the product of painstaking calibration by someone operating at the highest level of generative 3D craft.
